## 1. BACKGROUND AND RATIONALE

### 1.1 Disease Background

Essential hypertension is a major modifiable risk factor for cardiovascular disease, stroke, and chronic kidney disease. Approximately 1.13 billion adults worldwide are affected. Despite available therapies, blood pressure remains uncontrolled in over 40% of treated patients.

### 1.2 Investigational Product

**HTN-101** is a novel, once-daily, orally administered antihypertensive agent. HTN-101 50 mg has demonstrated dose-dependent reductions in both systolic and diastolic blood pressure in Phase II studies, with a favorable safety profile.

### 1.3 Rationale for Study

This Phase III trial is designed to confirm the efficacy and safety of HTN-101 50 mg compared with placebo over a 12-week treatment period in adults with essential hypertension.

---

## 2. STUDY OBJECTIVES AND ENDPOINTS

### 2.1 Primary Objective

To evaluate the efficacy of HTN-101 50 mg compared with placebo in reducing **systolic blood pressure (SBP)** after 12 weeks of treatment.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Change from baseline in mean sitting systolic blood pressure (SBP) at Week 12

### 2.2 Secondary Objectives

1. To evaluate the effect of HTN-101 50 mg on diastolic blood pressure (DBP)
2. To evaluate the safety and tolerability of HTN-101 50 mg
3. To evaluate the proportion of subjects achieving blood pressure control

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Change from baseline in mean sitting DBP at Week 12
- Proportion of subjects with SBP < 140 mmHg and DBP < 90 mmHg at Week 12
- Incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs)
- Change from baseline in pulse rate and body weight
- Incidence of abnormal laboratory values

### 2.3 Exploratory Endpoints

- Change from baseline in SBP and DBP at Weeks 4 and 8
- Time to blood pressure control

### 3.3 Randomization

Subjects will be randomized 1:1 to one of two treatment arms:

| Arm | Treatment | Dose | Route | Frequency |
|-----|-----------|------|-------|-----------|
| A | HTN-101 50 mg | 50 mg | Oral | Once daily (QD) |
| B | Placebo | 0 mg | Oral | Once daily (QD) |

Randomization will be performed using a centralized randomization system with a block size of 4, stratified by site.

### 3.4 Blinding

The study is **double-blind**: neither subjects nor investigators will know the treatment assignment. Matching HTN-101 and placebo tablets will be indistinguishable in appearance, taste, and packaging. Unblinding will occur only in medical emergencies or after database lock.

## 4. STUDY POPULATION

### 4.1 Inclusion Criteria

Subjects must meet **all** of the following:

1. Male or female, aged 18 to 75 years inclusive
2. Diagnosis of essential hypertension
3. Mean sitting SBP ≥ 140 mmHg and < 180 mmHg at Screening
4. Mean sitting DBP ≥ 90 mmHg and < 110 mmHg at Screening
5. Body mass index (BMI) between 18.0 and 40.0 kg/m²
6. Willing and able to provide written informed consent
7. Willing to comply with all study procedures and visit schedule

### 4.2 Exclusion Criteria

Subjects meeting **any** of the following will be excluded:

1. Secondary hypertension
2. Severe hypertension (SBP ≥ 180 mmHg or DBP ≥ 110 mmHg)
3. History of myocardial infarction, stroke, or transient ischemic attack within 6 months
4. Heart failure (NYHA Class III or IV)
5. Clinically significant arrhythmia
6. Type 1 diabetes mellitus
7. Uncontrolled Type 2 diabetes (HbA1c > 9%)
8. Chronic kidney disease (eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m²)
9. Clinically significant liver disease
10. Pregnant or breastfeeding women
11. Women of childbearing potential not using adequate contraception
12. Known hypersensitivity to HTN-101 or its excipients
13. Participation in another clinical trial within 30 days
14. Any condition that, in the investigator's opinion, would compromise the subject's safety or the study's integrity

### 4.3 Planned Sample Size

**100 subjects** (50 HTN-101, 50 Placebo)

Sample size rationale: Assuming a standard deviation of 12 mmHg for change in SBP and a treatment difference of 8 mmHg, 100 subjects (50 per arm) provide 90% power at a two-sided α = 0.05.

### 6.3 Blood Pressure Measurement Procedure

Blood pressure must be measured using a **calibrated, validated automated device** according to the following procedure:

1. Subject seated for **at least 5 minutes** before measurement
2. Arm supported at heart level
3. Appropriate cuff size (bladder encircling ≥ 80% of arm)
4. **Three measurements** taken 1–2 minutes apart
5. Mean of the three measurements recorded
6. Same arm (non-dominant) used at all visits
7. Measurements taken at approximately the same time of day

## 8. SAFETY ASSESSMENTS

### 8.1 Adverse Events

Adverse events will be collected from the time of informed consent through the End of Study visit. Severity (MILD/MODERATE/SEVERE), seriousness (Y/N), and relationship to study drug (NOT RELATED / POSSIBLY RELATED / RELATED) will be recorded.

### 8.2 Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)

An SAE is any AE that:
- Results in death
- Is life-threatening
- Requires hospitalization or prolongation of hospitalization
- Results in persistent or significant disability/incapacity
- Is a congenital anomaly/birth defect
- Is an important medical event

SAEs will be reported to the sponsor within 24 hours.

## 9. STATISTICAL METHODS

### 9.1 General Approach

All analyses will be performed using SAS® (version 9.4 or higher) and R (version 4.0 or higher). Continuous variables will be summarized using N, mean, SD, median, min, and max. Categorical variables will be summarized using counts and percentages.

### 9.2 Primary Efficacy Analysis

The primary endpoint (change from baseline in SBP at Week 12) will be analyzed using a **mixed-effects model for repeated measures (MMRM)** with:
- Fixed effects: treatment, visit, treatment × visit interaction, baseline SBP, site
- Random effect: subject
- Unstructured covariance matrix

**Treatment comparison:** HTN-101 vs Placebo, two-sided α = 0.05

### 9.3 Secondary Efficacy Analyses

- Continuous endpoints: MMRM, similar to primary
- Binary endpoints: logistic regression or Cochran-Mantel-Haenszel test

### 9.4 Safety Analyses

AEs will be summarized by MedDRA System Organ Class and Preferred Term. Laboratory shifts from baseline to worst post-baseline will be summarized.

### 9.5 Handling of Missing Data

- Primary analysis: MMRM (handles missing at random)
- Sensitivity analysis: Last observation carried forward (LOCF)
